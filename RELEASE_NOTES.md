**Bus Scope Community Edition is free to download and use.**

Bus Scope improves the USB investigation desktop with a resizable packet-details workspace and consistent capture controls.

- A bounded native command writes the acceptance fixture and reports its identity, so the installed GUI can open the same saved capture and inspect the sentinel USB payload.
- The resizable packet-details workspace preserves the traffic list, while capture controls and saved-session opening remain consistent; acceptance observes the same installed-build workflow.
